# Piimä

**Category:** Finnish mesophilic cultured milk\
**Region:** Finland\
**Milk:** principally cow milk in modern descriptions\
**Evidence status:** Nordic review plus one small Piimä-type production study; Finnish strain-resolved evidence remains limited

## Identity and production

Piimä is a Finnish cultured sour milk in the Nordic mesophilic tradition. Household cultures were transferred between batches; modern dairies use selected starters and heat-treated milk. Ordinary Piimä should be separated from ropy *pitkäpiimä*: the latter's long texture is a lineage-dependent culture trait, not a defining property of every Piimä. [VII010]

The catalog records an approximate **20 °C / 18–24 h** technical point from the product category review, rather than treating it as a surveyed national standard. A Brazilian study of a donated “Piima-type” culture used daily propagation, held culture and whole milk for **12 h at room temperature**, then refrigerated the analytical product at **4 ± 1 °C for 12 h** before adding honey. That protocol is useful evidence for one culture's handling, but it is neither a Finnish origin study nor a numeric ambient-temperature source. [PII001]

## Microbes, texture and acidification

Nordic descriptions associate Piimä with mesophilic *Lactococcus lactis* groups and aroma-forming lactococci or *Leuconostoc*. The Brazilian study referred to *Lactococcus* as the main culture group but did not publish a Finnish culture accession or a strain-resolved composition. Its plate-count method and product analysis do not define all Piimä communities. [PII001]

Ordinary Piimä is tart, smooth and drinkable. Ropy *pitkäpiimä* requires direct EPS or phenotype evidence for the culture under study. No obligatory yeast or mold, universal final pH, acidification rate or EPS-producing strain is established by the inspected sources.

## Propagation, storage and distinctions

Backslopping documents cultural continuity but can shift abundance and performance across transfers. The 12 h refrigeration step in PII001 is sample preparation, not a shelf-life trial; it provides no maximum storage duration or freeze/revival claim.

Piimä overlaps microbiologically with Filmjölk and cultured buttermilk, yet Finnish naming and ordinary-versus-ropy styles justify a separate record. Any commercial starter should be documented by its supplied taxa or strain certificates rather than assigned the general Piimä profile.

## Evidence limits

PII001 is a four-page, small study of a Piimä-type culture in Brazil, and its source provenance is not resolved. It supports only its stated process and measurements. A Finnish primary survey connecting household cultures, species, strain codes, EPS and acidification remains a priority gap.
