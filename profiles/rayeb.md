# Rayeb / Raib

**Category:** North African spontaneously set fermented milk and modern selected-culture analogue\
**Traditional region:** Morocco and Egypt; local processes differ\
**Alternate names:** Raib; Laban Rayeb\
**Milk:** traditionally cow milk in Moroccan accounts; camel, cow and mixed milk in the cited Egyptian starter study\
**Evidence status:** regional technical account, 42-sample Moroccan survey and defined Egyptian camel-milk starter experiment

## Identity and production

Moroccan Raib is spontaneously coagulated milk made in the same production chain as Lben and fresh cheese. A regional technical account describes raw milk held at room temperature, approximately **15–25 °C for 1–3 days**, with season-dependent timing. It may be eaten as the set product before churning. [LBE001]

Egyptian Laban Rayeb belongs to a related spontaneous-souring tradition but should not be treated as identical. Modern manufacture can heat milk and add yogurt or selected starters. One experimental study developed defined starters from Egyptian camel-milk isolates and tested them in camel, cow and 1:1 mixed milk; fermentation to the study endpoint varied with milk and starter, reaching **6.3–7 h** for some mixed-milk treatments and **18 h** for one camel-milk treatment. [RAY002]

## Sensory, acidification and microbial ecology

A survey of 42 Moroccan Raib samples reported mean pH **4.2**, lactic acid **0.67%**, and predominance of LAB with high coliform counts. These are survey averages, not recipe endpoints; the coliform finding is a hygiene observation rather than a defining microbiota. [RAY001]

Raib is acidic and softly coagulated and may separate whey. Natural inoculation, raw-milk microbiota and vessel carryover shape traditional batches. No obligatory yeast, mold or EPS organism is established for the whole category.

## Published isolates and EPS evidence

RAY002 formulated two experimental mixed starters using *Lactiplantibacillus plantarum* **FFNL146** and **FFNL159**, *Lactococcus lactis* **FFNL1926** and **FFNL2005**, and *Leuconostoc pseudomesenteroides* **FFNL2374**. FFNL1926/FFNL2005 were selected for strong acidification, FFNL146/FFNL159 for antimicrobial activity in the assays, and FFNL2374 for diacetyl and EPS production. The paper stored experimental products for 14 days under refrigeration. [RAY002]

These are real publication-specific isolate codes, but they came from sampled Egyptian camel milk and were assembled as experimental starters. They do not define traditional Moroccan Raib or every Egyptian Rayeb culture.

## Propagation, storage and distinctions

Spontaneous inoculation and vessel carryover are common; deliberate backslopping varies. The 14-day observation in RAY002 applies to its defined starter batches and does not establish a shelf life for raw spontaneously fermented Raib. Raw-milk hygiene, ambient temperature and post-set handling materially affect robustness.

Raib differs from churned [Lben](lben.md), thermophilic Zabadi and concentrated Labneh. Country, milk, inoculum and whether churning followed should accompany every record.

## Evidence limits

RAY001 supports average chemistry and broad microbiology in Moroccan samples but no strain identities. RAY002 supplies strain-level and functional evidence for a controlled Egyptian candidate starter; its strain traits should not be generalized beyond the reported assays.
