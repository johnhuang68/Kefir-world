# Zabadi

**Category:** Egyptian yogurt-like fermented milk\
**Region:** Egypt; related names in Sudan and the Middle East require qualification\
**Alternate names:** Zabady\
**Evidence status:** literature-backed traditional survey; modern process treated separately

## Origin and production

Traditional Egyptian Zabadi is commonly made from buffalo milk, cow milk or a mixture. The documented process boils milk, cools it to about **45 °C**, adds a portion of the previous day's product and incubates near **42 °C until coagulation**, then cools to limit further acidification. The study did not give one universal time, so the catalog records temperature and leaves duration endpoint-based. [ZAB001]

Modern manufacture may use pasteurized standardized milk and defined yogurt cultures. That improves reproducibility but does not show that the diverse artisanal ecology survives unchanged.

## Sensory and microbial ecology

Zabadi is firm like yogurt and can have a cooked-milk flavor. Eleven Alexandria products showed *Streptococcus thermophilus* as the main dominant species and *Lactococcus garvieae* next, with *L. raffinolactis*, *L. lactis*, *Leuconostoc citreum*, *Lactobacillus delbrueckii* subsp. *bulgaricus* and *L. johnsonii* also detected. [ZAB001]

Gel bands, PCR matches and product codes Z1–Z11 are not starter strain designations. The study does not establish an obligatory strain code. Other bacteria in market samples may reflect artisanal ecology or hygiene and should not be promoted as desirable starter organisms without functional and safety testing.

## Acidification, EPS, propagation and storage

Cooling after coagulation limits post-acidification. No representative pH curve or universal endpoint time is available. Firmness can result from heat treatment, buffalo-milk solids and acid gelation; no strain-linked EPS is required or proven by texture alone.

Daily backslopping is directly documented. It does not ensure constant community composition through successive batches. No universal refrigerated shelf life, freezing recovery or fungal starter is established.

## Distinctions and evidence quality

Zabadi is locally specific despite overlap with yogurt. Industrial thermophilic Zabadi can use a defined classical pair, while artisanal samples contain wider species diversity. Evidence is strong for the 11-brand bacterial snapshot and process description; strain identity, fungal ecology, kinetics and propagation stability remain open.
