# Pendidam

**Category:** skimmed, backslopped fermented milk\
**Region:** Far North and Adamawa, Cameroon\
**Milk:** cow milk in the cited process studies\
**Related intermediate:** Kindirmou, the whole fermented milk before skimming\
**Evidence status:** field-process characterization, vessel experiment and product-linked LAB isolate study

## Identity and history

Pendidam is a traditional Cameroonian fermented skim milk associated with pastoral production and women's milk-processing knowledge. The defining process feature is that milk first becomes a whole fermented milk, then cream/butter is removed and the remaining fermented skim phase is retained or fermented further. This distinguishes Pendidam from Kindirmou and from generic sour milk. [PEN001] [PEN002]

## Traditional and controlled production

A 2024 field survey published in 2026 documented filtration of raw milk, heating at **60–70 °C for 20–30 min**, cooling, inoculation with two to three spoonfuls of previous product (*kittoum*), and fermentation at **28–35 °C for 24–72 h** to form *nyalloundé*. Producers then churned it for about 40 min, removed butter (*lebol*) and obtained Pendidam. This is the catalog's primary numeric process range. [PEN001]

An Adamawa vessel experiment used sieved cow milk heated around **80 °C for 15 min**, cooled to about 45 °C, inoculated with old Kindirmou or Pendidam, and held **24 h at 24 ± 5 °C** to make Kindirmou. After skimming, the remaining milk underwent another **24 h at 24 ± 5 °C** to form Pendidam. Calabash, enamel and plastic vessels produced different sensory and microbial outcomes. These conditions are a second regional workflow, not values to average into the first. [PEN002]

## Sensory, acidification and composition

Pendidam is a sour, fluid-to-lightly viscous skimmed beverage. PEN001 reported pH **3.35–3.66** and LAB counts of **6.80–7.01 log CFU/mL** in sampled products. Sensory acceptability remained high in that survey, while the vessel study favored calabash-fermented samples. These findings are sample-specific and do not offset hygiene concerns. [PEN001] [PEN002]

No controlled acidification curve, universal titratable-acidity endpoint or verified EPS-producing organism is established. Churning, fat removal, continuing acidification and microbial metabolites all contribute to texture and flavor.

## Microbial ecology and published strains

A 2021 study of Pendidam and Kindirmou isolates identified representative LAB by gene sequencing after M13-PCR grouping. The retained isolates comprised *Pediococcus acidilactici* (68.6%), *Limosilactobacillus fermentum* (reported as *Lactobacillus fermentum*, 28.6%) and *Lactiplantibacillus plantarum* (reported as *Lactobacillus plantarum*, 2.8%). These percentages describe the study's isolate collection, not whole-community abundance. [PEN003]

Named product-linked isolates included *L. plantarum* **KE04** and *L. fermentum* **PE172**, which showed antifungal activity in the reported assays. The codes are publication-specific strains and do not define the traditional starter. Several *P. acidilactici* isolates and one *L. fermentum* isolate carried the tested **agdi** gene, illustrating why technological selection and safety assessment must be strain-specific. [PEN003]

PEN001 reported fungi, spore-formers, staphylococci, coliforms and sulfite-reducing clostridia in field samples, while presumptive *Salmonella/Shigella* were not detected. These results indicate sanitation problems and are not desirable or defining fermentation organisms.

## Propagation, storage and robustness

Backslopping with *kittoum* or older Pendidam/Kindirmou maintains the process, while vessel biofilms and household handling may also influence succession. The cited studies do not define a maximum stable transfer count, freeze/dry recovery or validated shelf life. High acidity does not neutralize the reported hygiene hazards. Modern development should pair well-characterized starters with clean water, sanitized vessels, controlled heating and refrigeration.

Pendidam differs from Kindirmou by skimming/churning stage and from cultured buttermilk by its regional process lineage and mixed household inoculum.

## Evidence limits

PEN001 is recent and process-rich but region-specific. PEN002 tests vessels under one experimental workflow. PEN003 gives isolate-level identities and in-vitro traits for pooled Pendidam/Kindirmou sampling; it does not establish population abundance or clinical probiotic effects.
